# Subscriptions and billing

A Kettlewick subscription delivers freshly roasted coffee on a schedule you choose.
You can change, skip, pause or cancel it at any time from Account > Subscriptions.

## Delivery frequency

Choose a delivery every 1, 2, 3 or 4 weeks. You can change the frequency, the coffee
and the bag size for any upcoming delivery.

Every subscription bag is 10% cheaper than the same bag bought once. Subscriptions
with two or more bags per delivery ship free.

## Skipping, pausing and cancelling

Skip a single delivery, pause your subscription for up to 3 months, or cancel it
entirely. Changes for next week's roast must be made before the weekly cutoff:
Thursday at 11:59 pm Eastern time. Orders after the cutoff are already being roasted
and will ship as planned.

Cancelling takes effect immediately and there is no cancellation fee. Your order
history, saved addresses and Kettlewick Rewards points stay in your account.

## When you are charged

Your card is charged on the morning your coffee is roasted, usually one or two days
before it ships. The date of the next charge is shown on the Subscriptions page.

We email you 30 days before any price change takes effect, so you always have time to
adjust or cancel.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google
Pay. We do not accept cheques, bank transfers or cryptocurrency.

To update your card, open Account > Payment methods, add the new card and set it as
the default. The next subscription charge uses the default card.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails we retry it 3 times over 6 days and email you after each attempt.
After the third failed attempt the subscription is paused, not cancelled, so you can
resume it as soon as you update your payment method.

## Invoices and receipts

Every order has a PDF invoice. Open Account > Order history and choose Download
invoice. Invoices show the sales tax we collected, which we charge in every state
that requires it.

## Promo codes

You can use one promo code per order. Promo codes apply to one-time orders only and
cannot be combined with the subscription discount. Codes are not case sensitive.

## Gift subscriptions

Gift subscriptions last 3, 6 or 12 months and are paid up front. They do not renew
automatically; we email the buyer two weeks before the last delivery in case they
want to extend it.

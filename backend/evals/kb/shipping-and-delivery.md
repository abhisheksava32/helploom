# Shipping and delivery

Kettlewick Coffee Co. roasts every bag to order in our roastery in Asheville, North
Carolina. This page explains when your order leaves us, how much shipping costs and
what to do if a parcel goes missing.

## Processing times

Orders placed before 1 pm Eastern time on a business day ship the same day. Orders
placed after 1 pm, on weekends or on public holidays ship the next business day.

We roast Monday to Thursday. Coffee always ships within 48 hours of roasting, so the
beans reach you fresh. Equipment orders (grinders, kettles and brewers) ship from our
warehouse and are not affected by the roasting schedule.

## Shipping rates

| Service | Delivery time | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3 to 5 business days | $5.95 |
| Express | 1 to 2 business days | $14.95 |

Standard shipping is free on orders over $45 after discounts. Subscriptions with two
or more bags per delivery always ship free. Express shipping is never free, except
for members of the Roast tier of Kettlewick Rewards.

We ship with UPS and USPS. The carrier is chosen automatically based on your address
and the weight of the parcel; we cannot ship with a carrier you pick.

## Tracking your order

You receive a tracking email within 24 hours of dispatch. You can also follow every
parcel from Account > Orders. Tracking can take up to one business day to show the
first scan after the label is printed.

## Changing the delivery address

You can change the delivery address yourself within 1 hour of placing the order, from
Account > Orders > Edit address. After that the order is already in our roasting
queue; contact support and we will try to update it with the carrier, but we cannot
promise the change.

## International shipping

We ship coffee to Canada, the United Kingdom, every country in the European Union,
Australia and New Zealand. International delivery takes 6 to 12 business days and
costs a flat $19.95 per order.

Customs duties and import taxes are set by the destination country and are paid by
the customer on delivery. We cannot mark parcels as gifts or declare a lower value.

Electric kettles and grinders ship only to the US and Canada, because their plugs and
voltage are built for North American outlets.

## PO boxes and signatures

Coffee and accessories can ship to PO boxes. Equipment cannot, because orders over
$250 need a signature on delivery.

## Lost or delayed parcels

If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, or the parcel is marked delivered but you
cannot find it, contact support. Please check with neighbours and your building's
mail room first. Once we confirm the parcel is lost we either reship the order at no
cost or refund it in full, whichever you prefer.

Returns and refunds

We want every cup to be a good one. If something is not right with your order, this policy explains what you can send back, how long you have and when your money comes back.

Return windows

Equipment such as grinders, kettles and brewers can be returned within 30 days of delivery if it is unused or only lightly used and comes back in its original packaging with all accessories.

Unopened bags of coffee can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Opened bags of coffee are not returnable, because we cannot resell them.

Items marked "Final sale" in our clearance section cannot be returned or exchanged.

The Love It Guarantee

If you do not enjoy a coffee you bought, tell us within 30 days and we will send you a different coffee of the same size for free. The guarantee applies once per order and you do not need to send the opened bag back.

How to start a return

  1. Go to Account > Orders and choose Return items next to the order.
  2. Select the items and the reason for the return.
  3. Print the prepaid return label we email you and attach it to the box.
  4. Drop the box at any UPS location within 14 days of printing the label.

A prepaid return label costs $7.50, which we deduct from your refund. Labels are free when the item arrived damaged or defective, or when we sent the wrong item.

Refunds

We process your refund within 3 business days of receiving the return at our warehouse. Refunds go back to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, it can take another 5 to 10 business days before the money shows on your statement.

If you prefer, you can take store credit instead of a refund. Store credit is issued instantly when the return is scanned and adds a 10% bonus to the refund amount.

Damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged, report it within 7 days of delivery with photos of the item and of the shipping box. We will send a replacement or refund you in full, and you will not pay for the return label.

Exchanges

We do not swap items directly. To exchange a grinder or kettle for another colour or model, start a return for the original item and place a new order for the one you want. That way the new item ships right away instead of waiting for the return.

Gifts

Gift recipients can return items too. The refund is issued as store credit to the recipient, so the person who bought the gift is never notified.

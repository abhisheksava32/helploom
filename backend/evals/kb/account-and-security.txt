KETTLEWICK ACCOUNT AND SECURITY GUIDE

This guide covers signing in, keeping your Kettlewick account safe, and managing or
deleting your personal data.

Signing in

You sign in with your email address and password. After 5 failed sign-in attempts in
a row, the account is locked for 15 minutes to protect it from guessing attacks. You
can still reset your password while the account is locked.

Resetting your password

Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and we email you a reset link. The link
is valid for 60 minutes and works only once. If it has expired, simply request a new
one.

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long. We recommend a passphrase of several
unrelated words, and we block passwords that appear in known data breaches.

Two-step verification

Two-step verification adds a 6-digit code from an authenticator app to every sign-in.
Turn it on in Settings > Security > Two-step verification and scan the QR code with an
app such as Google Authenticator, 1Password or Authy.

When you turn it on we show 10 backup codes. Each backup code works once. Store them
somewhere safe: if you lose your phone and your backup codes, support has to verify
your identity before removing two-step verification, which can take up to 3 business
days.

Changing your email address

Change your email in Settings > Profile. We send a confirmation link to the new
address, and the change only takes effect after you click it. Your old address gets a
notification, so you will know if someone else tried to change it.

Signing out everywhere

If you think someone else has access to your account, open Settings > Security and
choose "Sign out of all devices", then change your password.

Phishing and scams

Kettlewick will never ask for your password, your two-step code or your full card
number by email, chat or phone. If you receive a message like that, do not reply.
Forward it to security@kettlewick.example and delete it.

Downloading your data

You can request a copy of your personal data in Settings > Privacy > Download my
data. We prepare a ZIP file with your profile, orders and support conversations and
email you a download link within 48 hours. The link stays valid for 7 days.

Deleting your account

To delete your account, open Settings > Privacy > Delete account. Any active
subscription is cancelled first. Deletion is completed within 30 days. We keep order
and invoice records for 7 years because tax law requires it, but they are no longer
linked to a profile you can sign in to.
